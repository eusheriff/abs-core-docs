## 2. The Clearing House: The Economic Engine
ABS V6.5 introduces **"Proof of Compliance (PoC)"**.
An agent only receives hardware authorization to act after the micro-fee is processed.

### The Governance Toll Formula
The validation fee ($F$) for each intent is dynamically calculated:

$$F = B + (R \cdot \mu) + L_{tx}$$

Where:
*   $B$: Base processing fee (WASM Compute).
*   $R$: Risk Score of the action (0-100).
*   $\mu$: Sensitivity multiplier (e.g., Financial > Social Media).
*   $L_{tx}$: L2 Anchoring Fee.
